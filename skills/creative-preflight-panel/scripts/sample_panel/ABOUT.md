# Fictional sample panel

Everything in this folder is FICTIONAL: 12 buyer profiles for "Brand Z", an invented sleep routine app, written by Claude from invented ICP notes and from the audience signals in the fictional market snapshot of `competitor-creative-parser`. No real person is described.

- `profiles.jsonl`: the 12 profiles (7 from ICP notes, 5 from snapshot signals; p10 is a good sleeper kept as a control).
- `concepts.jsonl`: the 6 concepts of the fictional brief pack, written by `market-to-creative-brief`'s `brief_pack.py --panel-concepts` (3 static, 3 video with three hooks each, so 12 panel items).
- `live-run-2026-11-15/`: real Jev output (`jev-1.14.0`, direct endpoint) on these fictional concepts and profiles: answers per pair, run costs, order checks, the matrix and the verdicts. The concepts and profiles are invented; the answers and probabilities are real model output.
