# Fictional sample market

Everything in this folder is FICTIONAL: five invented sleep improvement apps ("Brand A" to "Brand E"), 12 static and 12 video ads, invented copy, invented numbers inside the ad copy, invented dates and transcripts. No real brand, advertiser or ad is shown. Landing URLs use the reserved example.com domain.

- `creatives.jsonl`: capture records in the format of `references/capture-and-access.md`.
- `descriptions.jsonl`: descriptions in the format of `references/description-templates.md`, as Claude would write them.
- `live-run-2026-11-15/`: real Jev output (`jev-1.14.0`, direct endpoint) on these fictional descriptions: tags, review queues, order checks, run costs and the snapshot. The ads are invented; the tags and probabilities are real model output.
